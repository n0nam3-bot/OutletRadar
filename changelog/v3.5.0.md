# Outlet Radar v3.5.0 — Privacy page, affiliate disclosure and basic site hygiene

## Why
Affiliate links and click counts are live, so the site needs to say plainly what it stores and how it earns money. This release adds those pages and some baseline hardening.

## What it adds
- **Privacy page** (`/privacy`): what is stored and what is not, written from what the app actually does. No accounts, no e-mail addresses, no cookies set by the site. Search words and filters go to the server to find results; anonymous daily counts of searches and clicks (no IP address, cookie or browser details; search counts deleted after 60 days); saved searches stay in the visitor's own browser; the watchlist stores the product details plus a random identifier created in the browser; hosting and database providers may keep technical logs; store links lead to third-party sites and affiliate networks that have their own policies.
- **Affiliate disclosure** (`/disclosure`): that some links are affiliate links and may earn a commission at no extra cost, that this does not change which prices are shown or how results are ordered, that prices and stock may be out of date, what "verified" and "listed" sizes mean, that independent marketplace sellers are not verified, and that the site is not affiliated with the brands or stores shown.
- **Footer links** to both pages on the main page.
- **`robots.txt`** (blocks `/api/` and `/admin/`) and **`sitemap.xml`** (home, privacy, disclosure). The sitemap and the Sitemap line appear only when `SITE_URL` is set, so wrong addresses are never published.
- **Security headers** on every response: `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, and a restrictive `Permissions-Policy`. No Content-Security-Policy is set: the page loads product images from many retailer domains, so a policy must be written against real traffic first.
- Optional settings that personalise the pages: `SITE_OPERATOR` (your name or business), `CONTACT_EMAIL` (validated, never rendered if malformed) and `SITE_URL`.

## Keep these pages true
The disclosure says affiliate relationships do not affect result order, and the privacy page lists exactly what is stored. If you change either (for example by ranking by commission, adding e-mail alerts or analytics), update the pages first. They are plain files in `app/privacy/page.tsx` and `app/disclosure/page.tsx`; the "last updated" date is in `lib/site-info.ts`.

These pages are a plain-language starting point written from the code, not legal advice. Before relying on revenue, check the rules that apply where you operate (affiliate disclosure and privacy requirements differ by country) and each affiliate network's and provider's terms.
