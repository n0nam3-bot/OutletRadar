# Outlet Radar v3.0.0 — Accurate size filtering with per-size prices

## What was wrong
- A size such as `10` matched both men's 10 and women's 10 when the audience filter was "All", although a women's 10 is a different shoe size.
- Any listed or quoted size counted as a match, with no distinction between "StockX has an ask at this size" and "a retailer lists this size".
- The price filter looked at the cheapest listing of any size, not the price of the size you asked for.
- Sizes were never converted between scales, so a women's 11.5 search could not find a men's 10 shoe.

## What changed
- **Size scales.** A new **Size type** control (Auto, Men's US, Women's US, Kids' (Y)) sits next to the size box. Auto follows the audience filter. A size can also carry its own marker: `W 8`, `10.5W`, `5Y`.
- **Strict matching.** Sizes are compared within the same scale. UK, EU and cm labels are ignored because they cannot be compared without a per-brand chart. Toddler "C" sizes and apparel sizes are handled separately.
- **Conversion only where it is known.** Men's and women's sizes are converted for Nike, Jordan and New Balance (women's = men's + 1.5) and adidas (women's = men's + 1). Other brands (Converse, ASICS, Vans and so on) are never converted. Kids' sizes are never converted. A converted match is labelled as converted on the card.
- **Verified vs listed.** A size is **verified** when StockX, GOAT or Flight Club reports an active ask or availability for it, and then shows its price. It is **listed** when a retailer lists the size but stock and price for that size are not confirmed. A size that a marketplace reports as unavailable excludes the product. A product with no size data at all is counted as "could not verify", not silently dropped.
- **Per-size price.** Cards show "Size 10 (men's): from $230 · GOAT ask". With a size selected, the min/max price filter applies to that size's price, and results sort with verified sizes first, cheapest size price first.
- **Labels without a scale** (just "10") are treated as men's only for a men's search; they never match a women's or kids' search.
- The filter logic moved from the page into `lib/catalog-filters.ts` and `lib/size-match.ts` so it can be tested.

## Not changed
- Live size prices still come from StockX and GOAT through PCKT, for the first 20 results, after you press **Apply refinements** (about 0.4 credit per source per batch). Retailers such as Nike.com and Foot Locker supply listed sizes only.

## Migration and configuration
- No database migration. No new variables.

## Validation
- Production build and TypeScript check pass.
- 29 unit checks for size parsing, matching, conversion and filtering (men's 10 no longer matches women's 10; sold-out sizes are excluded; the price limit uses the selected size's price; Converse is never converted; apparel sizes still work).
- The conversion offsets follow the brands' commonly published size charts; verify them against the charts you rely on.
