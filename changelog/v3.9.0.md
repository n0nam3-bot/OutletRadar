# Outlet Radar v3.9.0 — Optional tip link and support page

## What it adds
- **A quiet "Leave a tip" line under search results**, linking to your Streamlabs tip page (`https://streamlabs.com/artofreyes/tip` by default): "Free to use, no account needed. If it saved you money, you can leave an optional tip to help cover hosting and data costs." No pop-ups, no nagging, shown once per results list.
- **A Support link in the footer** and a **`/support` page** explaining what tips are for and what they do not do: tips are voluntary, unlock nothing, do not change prices or result order, are handled entirely by the tip service (the site never sees names or payment details), and are not tax-deductible charity donations.
- **Disclosures updated.** The privacy page has a Tips section (the tip service processes tips under its own policy; the click is counted as an anonymous outbound click) and the affiliate disclosure mentions tips. The sitemap includes `/support`.
- **Tip clicks appear in the admin "Outbound clicks" report** as "Tip jar", so you can see whether the line is being used.
- **`TIP_URL`** changes the link to another https page (Ko-fi, PayPal.me, Buy Me a Coffee and so on); `TIP_URL=off` hides every tip link everywhere. Anything that is not a plain https address is rejected and the link is simply not shown.

## Is it allowed on the free hosting?
- **Vercel:** its Fair Use Guidelines (page last updated 14 September 2026) say Hobby is for non-commercial personal use, that making affiliate linking the primary purpose of a site or showing advertisements counts as commercial use, and that asking for donations does not. A link out to a tip page does not process any payment on your site. If you ever collect payments on the site itself, run ads, or make affiliate links the site's main purpose, you would need the Pro plan.
- **Neon:** I found nothing in its plan documentation about donations; the free plan is positioned for prototypes and side projects. Check Neon's terms if the site grows into something commercial.
- Terms can change; the pages are linked in `docs/free-tier-operations.md`.
