# Outlet Radar v1.4

## Audience and size interpretation
- Product title and subtitle audience labels take priority over broad provider metadata. A product described as `Men's Shoes` is classified as Men even if the API's generic gender field says `Unisex`; youth taxonomy and Y-size labels still take priority.
- Size normalization accepts additional provider field variants and nested availability/status objects. Combined labels such as `M 11.5 / W 13` are matched against the selected audience's side of the size conversion.

## Stock signals
- Only explicit stock signals are treated as available or unavailable. Values such as `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`, `IN_STOCK` and `OOS` are interpreted as stock signals; generic product/SKU states such as `ACTIVE` remain unverified.
- Size chips distinguish online shipping available, unavailable, and unverified. Gray/dashed sizes are listed options without a verified shipping-stock signal. Store pickup availability is never inferred from online shipping status.
- Product cards explain when a size is listed but its stock status is not reported.

## Detail responses
- A product detail response with no size rows no longer overwrites a non-empty size list from search.
- Product detail response unwrapping supports nested `data.product` and `data.item` envelopes.
